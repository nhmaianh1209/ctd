What exactly do High, Medium and Low mean in this assessment?
Rating Definitions
Criteria for Performance and Potential classification
Performance
Group 1 · 2 years of data (FY25 + FY26)
High
Only HE (Highly Effective) or O (Outstanding) across both cycles — no lower rating in either FY25 or FY26.
Low
At least one NI (Needs Improvement) in either FY25 or FY26.
Medium
All remaining combinations — neither exclusively HE/O across both years, nor containing any NI.
Group 2 · 1 year of data (FY26 only)
High
O (Outstanding) in FY26.
Low
NI (Needs Improvement) in FY26.
Medium
HE (Highly Effective) or E (Effective) in FY26.
Potential (3 indicators)
High
Demonstrates all 3: strong growth motivation + high engagement + readiness for next-level roles.
Medium
Demonstrates 1–2 indicators, or shows inconsistent potential across evaluation cycles.
Low
Does not demonstrate any of the 3 potential indicators.
